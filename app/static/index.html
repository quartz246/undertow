    Qloo vs the actual 2024 result

    Inside states it worked, a little

    We fit the taste model to 2020, then sorted counties by how far their tastes sat from their 2020 vote. Counties whose tastes were bluer than their vote really did swing toward Democrats in 2024, and redder ones toward Republicans: steadily, but by fractions of a point. Bars show the swing beyond what the 2020 margin and county size explain; the whiskers are 95% intervals.

    Statewide it did not

    The same test for whole states: a state's taste gap did not predict its 2020→2024 swing (the line is flat to slightly backwards). That is why Qloo never moves a statewide call here; the polls do.

    Caveat for every backtest: Qloo's data describes tastes today, not in 2020, so these are not clean out-of-time forecasts. All models are scored on states they never saw.
